The idea

Useful, not accusatory

01

Estimate

Count recipients and attachment weight. Email Impact uses a deliberately modest model that accounts for the cost of sending a message plus the extra load of large attachments.

02

Translate

Show the result as CO2e, then make it relatable: a short car journey, a few hours of TV, or a phone charge.

03

Share softly

Add a small byline to the signature. It is a prompt to make the next email lighter, not a score for the person who sent it.

A deliberately modest model

The demo starts with a 4g baseline, then adds 2g per recipient and 5g per recipient for each MB of attachment data. The figures are intentionally visible in the downloaded script, so a team can replace them with a better model as evidence improves.
