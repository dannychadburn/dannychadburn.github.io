Before you book

A useful starting point

These examples count weekends and bank holidays as time off, so you only need to use annual leave on the weekdays listed. Christmas and New Year runs include the first January bank holiday where it extends the break.

Check your employer's holiday policy before booking. Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holidays, and substitute days can vary.
